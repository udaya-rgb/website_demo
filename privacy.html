Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 06 November 2026

Chenthur Jewellery ("we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect your information when you access or use the Chenthur Jewellery mobile application.

By using our application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you register and use our application:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Customer name
  • Mobile number
  • Residential address

1.2 Identity Documents

  • Identity or verification documents submitted by customers, where required for customer verification or scheme registration.

1.3 Account and Scheme Information

  • Customer account details
  • Selected jewellery savings schemes
  • Scheme registration details
  • Investment and payment records
  • Scheme maturity and redemption information

1.4 Payment Information

  • Online payment transaction references
  • Payment amount and transaction status
  • Payment confirmation details

Sensitive payment credentials, such as UPI PINs are handled by the relevant payment service provider and are not intended to be stored by Chenthur Jewellery.

1.5 Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information, such as device type, operating system, application version and diagnostic information, to maintain application performance and security, where applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To register and manage customer accounts.
  • To verify customer identity and information.
  • To register customers for jewellery savings schemes.
  • To manage scheme investments and payment records.
  • To process and verify online payments.
  • To display scheme details, payment history and maturity information.
  • To send payment confirmations, scheme updates and important notifications.
  • To provide customer support and respond to enquiries.
  • To prevent fraudulent activities and unauthorised access.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Online Payments

The Chenthur Jewellery application supports online payments through payment service providers.

When a customer makes a payment, relevant transaction information may be processed by the payment gateway to complete and verify the transaction.

We may receive and maintain transaction references, payment amounts, dates and payment statuses for customer account management, scheme administration and accounting purposes.

Customers should review the privacy policies and terms of the applicable payment service providers before making payments.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent customers' personal information to third parties.

We may share necessary information with:

  • Payment gateways and payment service providers for transaction processing.
  • Technology and hosting providers for application operations.
  • Customer communication and notification service providers.
  • Authorised employees and service providers who require access to perform their responsibilities.
  • Government authorities or legal agencies when required by applicable law.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that service providers handle personal information appropriately.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect customer information from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Access to customer information is restricted to authorised personnel who require it for legitimate business purposes.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain customer information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, manage jewellery savings schemes, maintain financial and transaction records, resolve disputes and fulfil legal obligations.

Certain payment, identity verification and scheme-related records may need to be retained even after an account deletion request, where required by applicable laws or legitimate business requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable retention requirements.

7. Account Deletion

Customers can request deletion of their Chenthur Jewellery application account through the account deletion option available within the app.

After receiving a deletion request:

  • We will verify the customer's request where necessary.
  • We will review any pending scheme, payment or contractual obligations.
  • We will process account deletion in accordance with applicable laws and requirements.
  • Personal information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymised.
  • Certain transaction, financial, identity verification and scheme records may be retained where legally required.

Deleting an app account does not automatically cancel an active jewellery savings scheme or remove outstanding financial obligations. Customers should contact Chenthur Jewellery for assistance with pending schemes before requesting deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for individuals who are legally eligible to participate in the applicable jewellery savings schemes.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent or authorisation required by applicable law.

9. Third-Party Services

Our application may use third-party services for payment processing, hosting, notifications and other operational purposes.

These service providers may process relevant information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

We encourage customers to review the privacy policies of the relevant third-party providers.

10. Customer Rights

Subject to applicable laws, customers may:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of their account and eligible personal information.
  • Raise concerns about the handling of their personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.

Customers may exercise these rights by contacting Chenthur Jewellery or using the relevant options available within the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Chenthur Jewellery reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our application, services, business practices or legal requirements.

Any revised Privacy Policy will be published on our official website or through the application with an updated effective date.
