This policy applies to personal information shared through this website, in our showroom, and through phone, WhatsApp, email or other direct communications with Chentur Jewellers.

Who we are

Chentur Jewellers is a fine jewellery business offering gold and bridal jewellery, along with gold savings and exchange schemes. References to “we”, “us” or “our” in this policy refer to Chentur Jewellers.

If you have a question about this policy or how your information is handled, please use the contact details at the end of this page.

Information we collect

We collect information that you choose to share with us, as well as limited information generated when you use this website.

Information you provide

  • Your name, phone number, email address and other contact details submitted through an enquiry form, WhatsApp message, phone call or in person.
  • Details of the piece, collection, scheme or appointment you are interested in.
  • Any message, preference or other information you include in your communications with us.
  • Information provided during showroom visits, scheme enrolment or related paperwork, where applicable.

Information collected automatically

Our website hosting or analytics tools may record limited technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location and pages visited. This information is used to understand how the website is used and to keep it working properly.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide information about jewellery, collections, availability and schemes.
  • Arrange and manage appointments, private viewings and scheme consultations.
  • Process scheme enquiries or related requests where you choose to proceed.
  • Improve our website, services and the way we communicate with visitors.
  • Meet legal, accounting or regulatory requirements that apply to our business.
  • Send updates or offers if you have chosen to receive them.

We do not sell personal information. We use the information we collect for the purposes described above and for purposes that are reasonably related to them.

When we share information

We may share limited personal information with service providers who help us operate our website, communicate with you, manage appointments or maintain our business records. These providers should only use the information for the services they provide to us.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect our legal rights, to prevent fraud, or as part of a business restructuring or transfer, subject to applicable law.

When you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your communication is also subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy terms and policies.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic functionality and, where enabled, to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, provide additional choices.

How we protect information

We aim to handle personal information with care and use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Please avoid sending sensitive financial or identity information through unsecured channels. For scheme enrolment and other formal processes, our team will explain the appropriate steps.

How long we keep information

We retain personal information for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, provide services, maintain business records and meet legal or regulatory obligations. When information is no longer needed, we aim to delete it or remove the details that identify you.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it for certain purposes. You may also withdraw consent for optional communications at any time.

To make a request, please contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Third-party links

This website may contain links to third-party websites or services, including WhatsApp and Google Maps. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. Please review their policies before sharing information with them.

Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Please review it periodically.
